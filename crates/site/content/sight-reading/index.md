Sight-reading rhythms means reading a figure once and playing it in time, without
practicing it first. The best way to get there is a lot of fresh material at the right
difficulty. grooph writes that material for you: pick a note value and a level, and every
tap on the dice (**New rhythm**) gives you a new exercise.

## A simple routine

1. **Pick the level** where you make a few mistakes, but not many.
2. **Look before you play.** Scan the exercise for rests and syncopations.
3. **Count in.** Switch on the count-in so you start in tempo.
4. **Don't stop.** If you miss a note, keep counting and come back in on the next beat.
5. **Next rhythm.** Generate a new one instead of repeating the old one.

Ten minutes a day with new material is more effective than an hour once a week.
