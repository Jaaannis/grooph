A metronome that always clicks can carry you without you noticing. grooph has two trainer
modes that make you keep the time yourself or push your tempo bit by bit. Both are in the
settings (gear icon) and work with any rhythm, the [rhythm generator](/rhythm-generator/)
and its endless mode included.

## Gap click: muted bars

Under **Gap Click**, switch on **Mute bars**. grooph then plays some bars and leaves others
silent, and you keep playing through the gap. When the click comes back you hear at once
whether you rushed or dragged.

- **Pattern** plays a number of bars, then mutes a number of bars, for example 2 on and 2
  off.
- **Random** mutes each bar with the chance you set. The first bar always sounds.
- **Click only** keeps the notes of your rhythm and only mutes the metronome.
  **Click and notes** silences everything.

A muted bar is dimmed on the staff, so you can see the silence is meant. The count-in is
never muted. With [timing practice](/timing-practice/) your hits in muted bars still count,
so the result shows how well you kept the time on your own.

## Tempo ramp: speed trainer

Under **Tempo Ramp**, switch on **Change the tempo while playing**. The ramp starts at the
BPM in the main menu and goes towards the target tempo in steps, for example 5 BPM every
4 bars or every loop of the rhythm. The tempo only changes at the start of a bar, and the
tempo being played is shown next to the BPM field.

At the target the ramp can **hold** the tempo, **start over** from the beginning or go
**back down** and up again. Tick **Keep the reached tempo after stopping** if the next run
should start where this one ended.

## Tips

- **Start a ramp at a tempo you can play cleanly** and use small steps. If it falls apart,
  stop, and let the next run start a little lower.
- **Begin with short gaps.** One muted bar after three audible ones is a good start;
  stretch the gap once it feels safe.
- **Count during the gap**, out loud or in your head. The [counting guides](/learn/) help
  with subdivisions.
